# National Engineering Works product illustrations

This directory contains 63 original SVG **concept illustrations** of equipment and processing plants. Each illustration is generated for the named machine category and is deliberately unbranded. These are **not photos of manufactured equipment**, certified machine specifications, or representations of specific installations. They are only for visual orientation while National Engineering supplies approved product-specific photographs.

No third-party company logos, vendor photographs, watermarks, or copied artwork are used. Replace the SVGs with photographs supplied or licensed for National Engineering Works, and verify actual available manufacturing scope before publication.
